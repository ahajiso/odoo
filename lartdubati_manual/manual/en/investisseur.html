Fiches for investors: the home page and the stock monitor. An investor only sees the stocks allowed by their access profile (ADM-11). Available on the test database artdubati_test, awaiting production.

INV-01Open the investor home page

Who · When: investor, at each login.

Prerequisites: an investor account (ADM-10).

Steps

  1. Log in to erp.lartdubati.com with your login.
  2. The home page opens with four buttons: Financial, Administrative, Commerce & Services, Production.
  3. Click Financial to open the stock monitor (INV-02).
  4. Administrative, Commerce & Services and Production show « Coming soon »: these functions are not open yet.
  5. To come back to the home page, open erp.lartdubati.com/odoo.

Verification: the four buttons show in the account's language.

See also: INV-02, INV-04, ADM-10.

INV-02View the stock monitor

Who · When: investor, to know what the stocks hold and what they are worth.

Prerequisites: a stock access profile assigned (ADM-11).

Steps

  1. From the home page, click Financial, or open Dashboards → Stock Monitor.
  2. The default view is a pivot table: stocks in rows, Asset and Consumable in columns, with Quantity and Inventory Value.
  3. For one stock: type its name in the search bar and choose « Search Stock for: … ».
  4. For a country, a city, a warehouse, a family or an ownership: same method, or open the search bar menu and pick a criterion under Group By.
  5. Click a row of the table (+ sign) to break it down, for example by Ownership.
  6. The icons at the top right switch to the list view (one line per item, totals per currency) or to the graph.

Verification: totals are grouped by Currency.

Warning: amounts in two different currencies are never added up; group by Currency before reading a total.

See also: INV-03, INV-04.

INV-03Understand the stock monitor columns

Who · When: investor, accountant, fleet manager, to read the stock monitor.

Column Meaning
Family Asset: an item tracked one by one in Maintenance (quantity 1). Consumable: a product whose quantity on hand is tracked.
Ownership Owned: company property, in its stock. Borrowed: belongs to a third party, lent free of charge. Rented: belongs to a third party, rent is paid. Lent Out: company property held by a third party.
Stock, Warehouse, Country, State, City Where the item is. Country and city come from the warehouse address (ADM-06).
Place Type Physical, Lent Out (stock of lent items) or Virtual.
Quantity 1 per asset; quantity on hand for a consumable.
Inventory Value Everything physically in the stock, whoever owns it. Company property: Cost on the equipment fiche, or average cost of the consumable. Third-party property: Replacement Value of the equipment, or replacement price of the consumable (ADM-12).
Accounting Value Company property only: net book value of the fixed asset (purchase value minus depreciation) for an asset, average cost for a consumable. 0 for third-party property.
Rent per Month, Rent Paid to Date Rented items only: rent converted to a monthly amount, and total of posted vendor bills (CPT-14).
Currency Currency of the stock's country; see the conversion setting in ADM-12.

Accounting Value and rent only appear in Dashboards → Stock Monitor - Values & Rent, reserved for the accountant and the stock manager (CPT-15).

See also: INV-02, CPT-15, ADM-12.

INV-04What an investor sees

Who · When: investor, administrator, to understand the rights of an investor account.

An investor account only sees the stock monitor. Its stock access profile limits the rows shown by country, stock, ownership and family; an empty list in the profile means no limit on that criterion; an account without a profile sees no stock. Accounting value and rent are not shown to it.

Steps (change language)

  1. Click your avatar at the top right, then Preferences.
  2. Choose the Language: French, English or Persian.
  3. Save: menus, buttons and columns change language.

Warning: stock and item names are entered data; they stay as entered, in every language.

See also: ADM-10, ADM-11.
