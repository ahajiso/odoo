CPT-01Fill in an equipment item's values

Who · When: accountant, after the fiche is created by the fleet manager and on receipt of the invoice.

Steps

  1. Open the fiche in Maintenance → Equipment.
  2. Product Information tab: Cost = pre-tax purchase price shown on the invoice.
  3. Third-Party Ownership (Shareholder) group: check Accounting Ownership (Company or Third Party).
  4. If Company and a durable capitalized asset: check Accounting Depreciation Active and follow CPT-03.
  5. If Third Party: fill in Replacement Value (amount owed in case of loss); leave Accounting Depreciation Active unchecked.
  6. Save.
Case Accounting Ownership Cost Accounting Depreciation Active Replacement Value
Purchase, durable capitalized asset Company Pre-tax price Checked Optional (insurance)
Purchase, small equipment expensed Company Pre-tax price Unchecked Optional
Shareholder contribution or loan, borrowing from a third party Third Party Empty Unchecked Mandatory
Rental Third Party Empty Unchecked Per contract

See also: REF-05, CPT-03, CPT-05.

CPT-03Capitalize an equipment item

Who · When: accountant, on entering the purchase invoice for a durable asset owned by the company.

Criterion: an asset used for more than a year and worth more than the threshold set by the company (the usual tax tolerance is €500 pre-tax; to confirm with the external accountant). Below that: direct expense in 6063 Fournitures d'entretien et de petit équipement.

Standard entries

When Debit Credit
Purchase invoice 2154 Matériel industriel (2182 vehicle, 2183 IT) + 44562 TVA sur immobilisations 404 Fournisseurs d'immobilisations
Annual depreciation charge 6811 Dotations aux amortissements 28154 (28182, 28183) Amortissements
Write-off (loss, theft, scrapping) 28154 + 675 2154

Steps

  1. Enter the vendor bill with the fixed-asset account on the line.
  2. Record the asset in the fixed-assets register, referencing the exact name of the equipment fiche.
  3. On the equipment fiche, write the asset and invoice reference in the history.

Warning: Odoo Community has no Fixed Assets module. Decision: install the OCA fixed-assets management module rather than keeping this tracking outside Odoo. Until it is installed, the fixed-assets register and the depreciation charges are kept outside Odoo or entered as manual journal entries. Never capitalize an asset whose Accounting Ownership is Third Party.

To do: install and configure the OCA module (depreciation periods per equipment category to be set with the chartered accountant — see the question raised at the meeting, REF-03).

Practical tip: the Site Machinery, Vehicles categories and large lifting or formwork equipment (REF-03) are the natural candidates for capitalization; hand tools and power tools usually stay under the threshold and go straight to expense (CPT-01).

See also: CPT-01, CPT-06, REF-08.

CPT-04Handle compensation owed to a third party

Who · When: accountant, when an equipment item belonging to a shareholder or a third party is lost, stolen or destroyed (PARC-08).

Steps

  1. Open the fiche; check Accounting Ownership = Third Party and read the Replacement Value.
  2. Ownership Status = Compensation Payable.
  3. Post the liability entry for the Replacement Value.
  4. On payment: payment entry, then Ownership Status = Compensation Paid.
  5. Ownership Status = Closed and archive the fiche.
When Shareholder owner Third-party owner
Liability recorded D 6788 Charges exceptionnelles diverses / C 455 Associés – comptes courants D 6788 / C 467 Autres comptes créditeurs
Payment D 455 / C 512 Banque D 467 / C 512 Banque

Alternative: if the company replaces the equipment instead of paying, Ownership Status = Replaced for Owner, and the purchase of the replacement follows CPT-02.

See also: PARC-06, PARC-08, REF-06.

CPT-05Rental, borrowing, loan: accounting treatment

Who · When: accountant, as soon as a contract or supporting document is received for an equipment item whose acquisition mode (REF-05) is not a purchase.

The acquisition mode (REF-05) determines the treatment; none of these cases is capitalized by us, except a loan of an asset that was already ours (Case 3).

Case 1 — Rental from a professional rental company (PARC-05)

Invoice received regularly, with VAT.

  • Entry on every invoice: D 6135 Locations mobilières + D 44566 TVA déductible / C 401 Fournisseurs
  • Any security deposit: D 275 Dépôts et cautionnements versés / C 512 Banque
  • Equipment fiche: Accounting Ownership = Third Party, Accounting Depreciation Active unchecked, Cost empty, Replacement Value per contract

Case 2 — Free loan received, from a shareholder or a third party (PARC-06)

Free provision, no invoice.

  • No entry on arrival: the asset stays off balance sheet, does not enter our assets.
  • If the company covers maintenance or insurance: D 6155 Entretien et réparations (or 616 Primes d'assurance) / C 401 or 512, as a normal expense.
  • In case of a claim: see CPT-04 (compensation owed to the owner).

Case 3 — Loan granted to a third party (PARC-07)

The asset remains ours; it keeps depreciating normally (CPT-03) even away from our premises.

  • Free loan: no entry related to the loan; only the depreciation continues.
  • Charged loan (rental granted to a third party): D 411 Clients + 44571 TVA collectée / C 708500 Locations diverses (produits accessoires), on every invoice issued.

Case 4 — Shareholder contribution

Three possible structures, to choose with the external accountant:

  • Free provision (the shareholder lends their own asset): treatment identical to Case 2, no entry, ownership stays with the third party.
  • Contribution via shareholder current account (the shareholder finances the purchase, the company becomes owner): D 2154 Matériel + 44562 TVA / C 455 Associés – comptes courants; later repayment D 455 / C 512.
  • Capital contribution in kind: requires a contribution deed and, depending on the amount, a contribution auditor; D 2154 / C 4562 Actionnaires – apports en nature, then C 101 Capital. Only to be done with the external accountant.

Warning: check on every fiche under rental or borrowing (Cases 1 and 2) that Accounting Depreciation Active is unchecked and Cost is empty; an asset lent to a third party (Case 3), on the contrary, keeps depreciating normally with us. All accounts cited are indicative (Plan Comptable Général) and to be validated by the external accountant.

See also: PARC-05, PARC-06, PARC-07, CPT-01, CPT-03, CPT-04, REF-09.

CPT-06Fully depreciated equipment still in service

Who · When: accountant, when an equipment item's depreciation schedule reaches its end.

Steps

  1. Do not write anything off: the asset stays recorded at its gross value, fully depreciated, as long as it is in use.
  2. On the fiche, leave Ownership Status and Current Location unchanged; note in the history « totalement amorti le ‹date› ».
  3. On scrapping: write-off entry (D 28154 / C 2154, no residual value), Ownership Status = Closed, archive the fiche.

Warning: a depreciated equipment item stays in the physical inventory (PARC-04). Do not archive it while it is still in use.

See also: CPT-03, REF-01.

CPT-07Record a general expense: bank fees, phone subscription, administrative fees

Who · When: accountant or Odoo admin — set up once, then on every invoice received.

Prerequisites: these accounts already exist in the imported chart of accounts (listed below); the product category and the 3 products were created on 26/09/2026.

Steps — one-time setup

  1. Create the expense accounts (if the external accountant does not already have their own): Invoicing → Configuration → Accounting → Chart of Accounts → New.

    • 627800 – Autres charges et commissions sur prestations de services (bank fees)
    • 626000 – Frais postaux et de télécommunications (phone subscription)
    • 606400 – Fournitures administratives (administrative fees)

    (existing accounts from the imported Plan Comptable Général, reused as-is rather than creating new accounts — see also CPT-02)

  2. Create a dedicated product category: Inventory → Configuration → Product Categories → New → « Frais généraux » under All / Expenses (see REF-04).

  3. Create the 3 Service-type products: Sales (or Purchase) → Products → New, for each:

    • Name: Frais bancaires / Abonnement téléphonique / Frais administratifs
    • Product Type: Service
    • Purchase: checked — Sales: unchecked
    • Product Category: Frais généraux
    • Accounting tab → Expense Account: the account created in step 1 that corresponds

Steps — everyday use

  1. On every vendor bill (Invoicing → Vendors → Bills → New), add a line with the corresponding product; the expense account fills in automatically.

Verification: in Invoicing → Accounting → Journal Items, a filter on the account shows the amounts under the right account.

Warning: these 3 items are general company expenses, unrelated to any fleet equipment — not to be confused with CPT-01 to CPT-06 (equipment accounting).

See also: REF-04

CPT-08Costs incurred before the company's registration and launch

Who · When: accountant or director, for expenses paid by a founder before the company has its SIRET, or during the incorporation period.

Legal principle: a company only legally exists once registered with the RCS (obtaining the SIRET). Acts and expenses incurred before that date (registry fees, legal notice, fees for drafting the articles of association, capital deposit, first purchases or subscriptions) are deemed made by the founder(s) in their own name, « pour le compte de la société en formation ».

Steps

  1. Keep all invoices and receipts in the founder's name (ideally already worded « Société en formation, [Name], SIRET en cours »).
  2. List these acts and commitments in a schedule attached to the articles of association and signed before registration — a condition for their automatic assumption by the company. Failing that, an express assumption by shareholder resolution after registration is required.
  3. Once the company is registered: reimburse the founder, posting the normal entry through the relevant expense account, offset by the shareholder current account: D 6xxx (expense) + 44566 TVA if recoverable / C 455 Associés – comptes courants; then on reimbursement D 455 / C 512 Banque.
  4. Post these entries in the first accounting period, the one starting at registration — a period cannot start before the company's legal existence.

Treatment of the costs themselves: current practice is to expense them directly in the period (accounts 6226 Honoraires, 6227 Frais d'actes et de contentieux, 6231 Annonces et insertions, 6064 Fournitures administratives, etc.) rather than capitalizing them under account 201 Frais d'établissement. Capitalization under account 201 (depreciable over 5 years maximum) remains legally possible but has become the exception — to decide with the external accountant depending on the amounts involved.

Warning: this subject touches both company law and accounting — to be validated with the external accountant and, for the assumption of acts, with whoever drafted the articles of association.

See also: CPT-07.

CPT-09Choose the VAT rate for a site

Who · When : manager or accountant, when drawing up each quotation, before entering the lines.

Principle : the rate depends on the nature of the site, not on the type of line. Labour and materials supplied and fitted as part of the same works fall under the same rate.

Rate Case Condition
20 % New build, commercial premises, consulting invoiced on its own Standard rate
10 % Renovation of a dwelling completed more than two years ago Certifying statement on the quotation and the invoice
5.5 % Energy improvement works Certifying statement, eligible works

Steps

  1. On each quotation line, check the tax proposed by the product and replace it if the site falls under another rate.
  2. For a reduced-rate site, check that the certifying statement appears on the quotation (see ADM-09).
  3. Create the invoice from the quotation: the rate carries over automatically.

Check : in Taxes, the 20 %, 10 % and 5.5 % rates are active, in both G (goods) and S (services) versions.

Warning : the 10 % and 5.5 % rates are archived by default in Odoo; they were reactivated by the lartdubati_facturation module. The choice between VAT on debits and VAT on payments has not been settled yet — to be confirmed by the chartered accountant.

See also : CPT-10, ADM-09.

CPT-10Invoice as a subcontractor

Who · When : accountant, on the first invoice issued to a construction main contractor.

Legal principle : in subcontracted construction works, VAT is owed by the main contractor and not by the subcontractor (art. 283-2 nonies of the French tax code). The invoice is issued excluding tax, with a reverse-charge statement.

Steps

  1. On the main contractor's contact record, tab Sales & Purchase, set Fiscal Position to Sous-traitance BTP - Autoliquidation (Art. 283-2 nonies CGI). Full list: Fiscal Positions.
  2. Create the invoice as usual from Customer Invoices: the line taxes switch to 0 % automatically.
  3. Check that the total tax is nil before confirming.

Check : the PDF carries the statement « Autoliquidation - Article 283-2 nonies du CGI. TVA due par le preneur (client donneur d'ordre). »

Warning : the VAT return boxes (CA3) are not filled in on these taxes yet. Until they are, the VAT return must be checked by hand. To be completed once the chartered accountant has confirmed them.

See also : CPT-09, CPT-11.

CPT-11Record a subcontractor's invoice

Who · When : accountant, when a construction subcontractor's invoice is received.

Legal principle : L'Art du Bâti accounts for the subcontractor's VAT itself. It is declared both as collected and as deductible: the cash effect is nil, but the return must show it.

Steps

  1. On the subcontractor's contact record, set Fiscal Position to Sous-traitance BTP - Autoliquidation (Art. 283-2 nonies CGI).
  2. Enter the bill from Vendor Bills: the tax switches to Autoliquidation sous-traitance BTP at the matching rate.
  3. Check the proposed rate and correct it if the works fall under another one (20 %, 10 % or 5.5 %).
  4. Pay the subcontractor the amount excluding tax.

Check : in the Journal Items tab of the bill, two VAT lines of the same amount and opposite sign (445662 collected, 44521 deductible).

Warning : require the subcontractor's URSSAF compliance certificate before any payment, and renew it as often as the chartered accountant advises. CA3 boxes still to be completed, see CPT-10.

See also : CPT-10.

CPT-12Import a bank statement and reconcile

Who · When : accountant, for each bank statement.

Prerequisites : the OCA reconciliation and import modules installed (account_reconcile_oca, account_statement_import_ofx and camt).

Steps

  1. Download the statement from the bank, in OFX or CAMT format.
  2. On the Invoicing dashboard, on the Bank journal card, choose Import, upload the file, then Import and start reconciliation.
  3. Reconcile each line against the matching invoice. For a line with no invoice, pick the counterpart account directly.

Check : the Bank journal balance matches the closing balance of the statement.

Warning : do not reconcile a line whose counterpart is uncertain. Leave it pending and settle it with the chartered accountant.

See also : CPT-13.

CPT-13Export the FEC file

Who · When : accountant, at year-end close or at the request of the tax authorities.

Principle : the FEC (accounting entries file) is compulsory in the event of a tax audit. The wizard ships with Odoo Community, but its menu was added by the lartdubati_facturation module.

Steps

  1. Open Invoicing → Reporting → Export FEC.
  2. Set the period, leave Test file ticked for a first run, then Generate.
  3. Check the resulting file, then run it again with Test file unticked for the final export.

Check : the file is plain text, UTF-8 encoded, columns separated by pipe characters.

Warning : outside test mode, the export sets the accounting lock date to the end of the period. Earlier entries can no longer be changed.

See also : CPT-12.

CPT-14Record the rent of a supplier contract

Who · When: accountant, at each due date of a rental contract (PARC-11).

Prerequisites: test database artdubati_test (awaiting production); a contract in Invoicing → Vendors → Supplier Contracts.

Steps

  1. Every day, Odoo automatically creates the bills of contracts that are due, as drafts.
  2. Open Invoicing → Vendors → Bills and filter on drafts.
  3. Check the bill against the lessor's document: amount, VAT, Bill Date.
  4. Confirm the bill.

Verification: the item's Rent Paid to Date goes up in Dashboards → Stock Monitor - Values & Rent.

Warning: only confirmed bills count in the rent paid. The entry follows CPT-05, Case 1 (account 6135, to be validated by the chartered accountant).

See also: PARC-11, CPT-05, CPT-15.

CPT-15Read the stock values and rent

Who · When: accountant, at each closing or on request.

Prerequisites: test database artdubati_test (awaiting production).

Steps

  1. Open Dashboards → Stock Monitor - Values & Rent.
  2. Read the Accounting Value per stock: company fixed assets and consumables at average cost.
  3. Read Rent per Month and Rent Paid to Date of rented items.
  4. Group by Currency before reading a total.

Verification: the total of Owned consumables matches the stock valuation in the accounts.

Warning: an asset's accounting value comes from its fixed asset; it is only known for equipment created from a vendor bill whose line carries the fixed asset. Otherwise it is 0. The asset profiles await the chartered accountant's validation.

See also: INV-03, CPT-03, ADM-12.
