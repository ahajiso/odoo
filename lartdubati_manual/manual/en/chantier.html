Site Supervisor / Worker

Three things to do at every movement or incident. They take less than a minute and prevent losing track of equipment.

CH-01Check out an equipment item for a site

Who · When: site supervisor or worker, when the equipment leaves the depot or another site.

Steps

  1. Open Maintenance → Equipment and search for the equipment (name or serial number).
  2. In the Location group, change Current Location to the destination site.
  3. Save.

Warning: do not touch any other field. If the site does not appear in the list, ask the fleet manager to create it (ADM-03).

See also: PARC-03.

CH-02Return an equipment item to the depot

Who · When: site supervisor or worker, when the equipment comes back.

Steps

  1. Open the equipment fiche.
  2. Current Location = the depot (WH/Stock).
  3. Save.
  4. If the equipment comes back damaged or incomplete, also do CH-03.

See also: PARC-03, CH-03.

CH-03Report a breakdown or damage

Who · When: anyone who notices a breakdown, damage, a loss or a theft, the same day.

Steps

  1. Open the equipment fiche and click the Maintenance button at the top.
  2. Click New.
  3. Request title: what happened, in a few words, e.g. « Moteur ne démarre plus » or « Disparu du chantier ».
  4. In the description: date, place, circumstances, photo if possible.
  5. Save.

Warning: do not change the Ownership Status; the fleet manager handles that (PARC-08). In case of theft, also notify the fleet manager immediately by phone.

See also: PARC-08.
